I built a Growth Decision Intelligence prototype for Careem Food that turns raw performance data into an executive-ready decision brief. It combines KPI trends, configurable business guardrails, city performance, customer lifecycle signals and experiment design. Instead of only describing what changed, it prioritises the most important business signal, explains the evidence, recommends one next action, and defines how success should be measured. The prototype uses fully synthetic data, so no confidential information is involved. I kept the metric and prioritisation layer transparent and auditable, while designing it so an LLM can later add richer root-cause hypotheses, segmentation analysis and executive storytelling without inventing data.

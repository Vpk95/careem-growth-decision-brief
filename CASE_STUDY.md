# Careem Growth Decision Intelligence — Case Study

## Problem
Growth teams can spend too much time converting dashboards into decisions. The challenge is not only identifying what moved, but deciding what deserves attention and what action should happen next.

## Solution
A Decision Brief Generator that turns raw growth metrics into:
1. Executive KPI snapshot
2. Guardrail breaches
3. Prioritised business signals
4. Recommended action
5. Experiment design
6. Success/guardrail metrics

## Why this fits a Growth Manager
The prototype mirrors the operating rhythm of a growth team: plan vs actual, funnel health, segmentation, root-cause thinking, growth initiatives, experimentation and revenue accountability.

## Product principles
- Evidence before opinion
- Incremental impact before vanity metrics
- Transparent reasoning
- One clear next action
- Every recommendation has a measurable KPI
- Human decision-maker remains accountable

## AI extension
The current demo uses transparent deterministic logic so every insight can be audited. An LLM can be added as a reasoning/narrative layer for root-cause hypotheses, segment comparisons and executive writing, while the metric calculations remain deterministic.

## Example output
Revenue growth below plan + CAC above guardrail → prioritise conversion/retention and reallocate inefficient acquisition spend → run a controlled 2-week test → measure incremental orders/revenue and contribution margin.

## Data
All included data is synthetic and created specifically for this challenge. No confidential Careem information is used.
